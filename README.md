
# 🍽️ Swiggy Sales Data Analysis Dashboard


**Tools Used:** Microsoft Excel | Power BI

## 📌 Business Requirement Document (BRD)

### 🧩 Problem Statement

In a highly competitive food delivery market, understanding sales performance, customer preferences, and regional demand patterns is critical for sustained growth.

This project aims to analyze **Swiggy’s sales data** and build interactive dashboards that enable stakeholders to:

* Track revenue performance over time
* Identify high-performing regions and cities
* Understand customer ordering behavior
* Evaluate customer satisfaction through ratings
* Support strategic, data-driven decision-making

---

### 📊 Business Questions

The dashboards are designed to answer key analytical questions:

* How does sales performance vary across months, weeks, and days?
* Which food category (Veg vs Non-Veg) drives the most revenue?
* Which states and cities contribute the highest sales?
* How do customer ratings correlate with sales performance?
* Are there any seasonal or weekly demand patterns?

---

### 📌 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

| KPI                         | Description                  |
| --------------------------- | ---------------------------- |
| **Total Sales (₹)**         | Overall revenue generated    |
| **Total Orders**            | Volume of orders processed   |
| **Average Order Value (₹)** | Revenue per transaction      |
| **Average Rating**          | Customer satisfaction metric |
| **Ratings Count**           | Engagement through reviews   |

---

## 📊 Dashboard Solution

This project includes **two dashboards** built on the same dataset to demonstrate analytical consistency across tools.

### 🔹 Power BI Dashboard

* Advanced interactive visuals with slicers
* Data modeling and DAX-based calculations
* Dynamic trend analysis (Monthly, Weekly, Daily)
* State-level and restaurant-level performance tracking

---

### 🔹 Excel Dashboard

* Built using Pivot Tables and Charts
* Structured KPI reporting
* Map visualization for geographical insights
* Quarterly performance tracking

---

## 📈 Key Insights & Findings

### 💰 Revenue Trends

* Sales show a **sharp decline in February**, followed by a **steady recovery and upward trend** through subsequent months
* Peak sales are observed around **May and August**, indicating possible seasonal demand or campaign impact

---

### 📅 Weekly & Daily Patterns

* **Weekends (especially Saturday)** generate the highest revenue, indicating strong leisure-driven demand
* Weekday sales remain relatively stable, showing consistent baseline demand

---

### 🍽️ Category Performance

* **Non-Veg category contributes ~64% of total revenue**, significantly outperforming Veg
* Indicates a strong customer preference toward non-vegetarian options

---

### 🌍 Regional Insights

* **Karnataka emerges as the top-performing state**, contributing the highest revenue
* Other strong markets include **Uttar Pradesh, Telangana, and Maharashtra**
* Sales distribution suggests concentration in urban and high-density regions

---

### 🏙️ City-Level Performance

* **Bengaluru leads among cities**, followed by Lucknow and Hyderabad
* Highlights the importance of metro and tier-1 cities in revenue generation

---

### 🍔 Restaurant Performance

* **Top brands like KFC and McDonald's dominate sales contribution**
* Suggests strong brand influence and customer loyalty toward established chains

---

### ⭐ Customer Behavior

* Average rating of **4.34** indicates overall positive customer satisfaction
* High rating count (~5.59M) reflects strong user engagement and platform activity
